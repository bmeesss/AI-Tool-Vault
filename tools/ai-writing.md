# AI Writing Tools

> Pricing snapshot verified against official websites on 2026-08-19. Prices can vary by region, billing cycle and promotions.

## ChatGPT

- **Official website:** https://chatgpt.com/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** General writing, brainstorming, research and rewriting
- **Description:** OpenAI's general-purpose AI assistant for writing, research, analysis, coding and multimodal tasks.
- **Why useful:** It covers a broad range of everyday writing and research workflows in one tool.
- **Tags:** `chatbot` `writing` `research` `brainstorming` `general-ai`

## Claude

- **Official website:** https://claude.ai/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Claude Pro is $20/month or $17/month with annual billing
- **Best application:** Long-form writing, document analysis and structured reasoning
- **Description:** Anthropic's AI assistant for conversation, analysis, writing, research and working with substantial amounts of text.
- **Why useful:** It is particularly strong for careful reading, structured writing and longer analytical tasks.
- **Tags:** `chatbot` `writing` `analysis` `long-context` `research`

## Grammarly

- **Official website:** https://www.grammarly.com/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + Pro; Pro is $30/month or $12/member/month billed annually
- **Best application:** Grammar, clarity, tone and writing improvement
- **Description:** A writing assistant that checks and improves text for correctness, clarity, tone and style across supported apps and editors.
- **Why useful:** It works close to where you already write, making polishing emails, schoolwork and professional text fast.
- **Tags:** `grammar` `editing` `proofreading` `tone` `writing-assistant`

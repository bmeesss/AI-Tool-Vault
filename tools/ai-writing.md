# AI Writing Tools

## ChatGPT

- **Official website:** https://chatgpt.com/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** General writing, brainstorming, research and rewriting
- **Description:** A general-purpose AI assistant that can draft, rewrite, summarize, explain and transform text while working with many other types of content.
- **Why useful:** One tool can cover a wide range of everyday writing and research tasks, making it a strong general-purpose starting point.
- **Tags:** `chatbot` `writing` `research` `brainstorming` `general-ai`

## Claude

- **Official website:** https://claude.ai/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** Long-form writing, document analysis and structured reasoning
- **Description:** Anthropic's AI assistant designed for conversation, analysis, writing and working with substantial amounts of text.
- **Why useful:** It is particularly useful when a task requires careful reading, structured writing or analysis of longer material.
- **Tags:** `chatbot` `writing` `analysis` `long-context` `research`

## Grammarly

- **Official website:** https://www.grammarly.com/
- **Category:** AI Writing
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** Grammar, clarity, tone and writing improvement
- **Description:** A writing assistant that checks and improves text for correctness, clarity, tone and style across supported apps and editors.
- **Why useful:** It works close to where you already write, so polishing emails, schoolwork and professional text is quick.
- **Tags:** `grammar` `editing` `proofreading` `tone` `writing-assistant`

# AI Image Tools

## Midjourney

- **Official website:** https://www.midjourney.com/
- **Category:** AI Image
- **Pricing model:** Paid plans
- **Best application:** High-quality creative image generation
- **Description:** A generative image platform focused on creating detailed and stylized visuals from text prompts.
- **Why useful:** It is a strong choice for visual concepts, artwork, thumbnails, moodboards and creative experimentation.
- **Tags:** `image-generation` `art` `design` `creative` `text-to-image`

## Adobe Firefly

- **Official website:** https://firefly.adobe.com/
- **Category:** AI Image
- **Pricing model:** Free access + paid plans
- **Best application:** Generative design and image editing
- **Description:** Adobe's generative AI platform for creating and editing visual content, including image generation and generative editing workflows.
- **Why useful:** It fits naturally into Adobe-oriented creative workflows and is useful for controlled design edits and generation.
- **Tags:** `image-generation` `image-editing` `adobe` `design` `generative-fill`

## Ideogram

- **Official website:** https://ideogram.ai/
- **Category:** AI Image
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** Posters, logos, graphics and images containing readable text
- **Description:** An AI image generator known for handling typography and text inside generated images particularly well.
- **Why useful:** It is useful when the visual itself needs headlines, labels, poster text or other prominent typography.
- **Tags:** `image-generation` `typography` `posters` `logos` `design`

# AI Productivity Tools

> Pricing snapshot verified against official websites on 2026-08-19. Prices can vary by region, billing cycle and promotions.

## Notion

- **Official website:** https://www.notion.com/
- **Category:** AI Productivity
- **Pricing model:** Free + Plus $10/member/month + Business $20/member/month; AI capabilities are included as trials on Free and Plus
- **Best application:** Notes, documentation, planning and workspace organization
- **Description:** A workspace platform for documents, databases, project information and team knowledge with integrated AI capabilities.
- **Why useful:** It combines information storage and productivity workflows instead of keeping notes, tasks and documentation in separate systems.
- **Tags:** `notes` `productivity` `workspace` `documentation` `knowledge-management`

## Zapier

- **Official website:** https://zapier.com/
- **Category:** AI Productivity
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Professional starts at $19.99/month and Team at $69/month
- **Best application:** Automating repetitive workflows between apps
- **Description:** An automation platform that connects applications and supports AI-powered steps, agents and workflow automation.
- **Why useful:** It can remove repetitive manual work by moving information and triggering actions between services automatically.
- **Tags:** `automation` `workflows` `integrations` `no-code` `ai-automation`

## Perplexity

- **Official website:** https://www.perplexity.ai/
- **Category:** AI Productivity
- **Pricing model:** Free + Pro; Pro is $17/month when billed annually
- **Best application:** AI-powered web research and information discovery
- **Description:** An AI search and answer engine designed to research questions and present answers with supporting sources.
- **Why useful:** It is useful for quickly exploring unfamiliar topics and starting research from a conversational search interface.
- **Tags:** `research` `search` `web` `citations` `knowledge`

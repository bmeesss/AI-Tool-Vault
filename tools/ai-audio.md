# AI Audio Tools

> Pricing snapshot verified against official websites on 2026-08-19. Prices can vary by region, billing cycle and promotions.

## ElevenLabs

- **Official website:** https://elevenlabs.io/
- **Category:** AI Audio
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Free includes 10,000 credits/month, Starter $6/month, Creator $22/month
- **Best application:** AI voice generation and speech workflows
- **Description:** A voice AI platform for generating natural-sounding speech and working with voice-related AI capabilities.
- **Why useful:** It is useful for narration, voiceovers, prototypes, accessibility and other speech-generation workflows.
- **Tags:** `text-to-speech` `voice-ai` `voiceover` `audio` `narration`

## Suno

- **Official website:** https://suno.com/
- **Category:** AI Audio
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Free $0, Pro $8/month and Premier $24/month when billed annually
- **Best application:** AI music generation
- **Description:** A generative music platform for creating songs and musical ideas from natural-language prompts.
- **Why useful:** It makes music ideation and rapid song prototyping accessible without traditional production software.
- **Tags:** `music-generation` `songs` `audio` `creative` `generative-music`

## Descript

- **Official website:** https://www.descript.com/
- **Category:** AI Audio
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Hobbyist $16/month annually or $24 monthly
- **Best application:** Transcript-based audio and video editing
- **Description:** A media editor built around transcription, allowing creators to edit recorded content through text alongside audio and video tools.
- **Why useful:** Editing by transcript can make cutting spoken content much faster than traditional timeline-only editing.
- **Tags:** `audio-editing` `video-editing` `transcription` `podcasting` `creator-tools`

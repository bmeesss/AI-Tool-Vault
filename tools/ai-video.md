# AI Video Tools

> Pricing snapshot verified against official websites on 2026-08-19. Prices can vary by region, billing cycle and promotions.

## Runway

- **Official website:** https://runwayml.com/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Free tier with 125 one-time credits + paid plans
- **Best application:** Generative video and creative production
- **Description:** A creative AI platform for generating and manipulating video with generative models and production-oriented tools.
- **Why useful:** It combines generation and creative control for short-form content, concepts and professional visual production.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `text-to-video` `creative` `video-editing` `generative-video`

## Pika

- **Official website:** https://pika.art/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans; Basic has 80 monthly video credits and Standard is $10/month
- **Best application:** Short AI videos, effects and social content
- **Description:** An AI video creation platform focused on turning prompts and images into short generated clips, animations and effects.
- **Why useful:** It is well suited to fast experiments and attention-grabbing short-form videos.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `effects` `short-form` `social-media` `image-to-video`

## Luma

- **Official website:** https://lumalabs.ai/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Free trial credits + Plus, Pro and Ultra plans; additional credits can be purchased
- **Best application:** Cinematic video generation and creative workflows
- **Description:** Luma is a creative AI platform with Ray video generation, image generation and agentic creative workflows in its consumer app.
- **Why useful:** It combines video, image and creative-agent workflows in one environment and supports detailed control over generated media.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `text-to-video` `image-to-video` `creative-ai` `ray`

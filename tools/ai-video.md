# AI Video Tools

## Runway

- **Official website:** https://runwayml.com/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Free access + paid plans
- **Best application:** Generative video and creative production
- **Description:** A creative AI platform for generating and manipulating video with generative models and production-oriented tools.
- **Why useful:** It combines generation and creative control, making it useful for short-form content, concepts and professional visual production.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `text-to-video` `creative` `video-editing` `generative-video`

## Pika

- **Official website:** https://pika.art/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Free + paid plans
- **Best application:** Short AI videos, effects and social content
- **Description:** An AI video creation platform focused on turning prompts and images into short animated or generated clips and effects.
- **Why useful:** It is well suited to fast experiments and attention-grabbing short-form videos.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `effects` `short-form` `social-media` `image-to-video`

## Sora

- **Official website:** https://sora.com/
- **Category:** AI Video
- **Pricing model:** Access and pricing vary by plan
- **Best application:** Generative video from text and visual concepts
- **Description:** OpenAI's generative video experience for creating video content from natural-language instructions and supported inputs.
- **Why useful:** It can turn detailed visual ideas into video concepts without a traditional filming workflow.
- **Tags:** `video-generation` `text-to-video` `openai` `creative` `generative-video`
